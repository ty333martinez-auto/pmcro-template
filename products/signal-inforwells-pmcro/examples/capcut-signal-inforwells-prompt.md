# CapCut Prompt — Signal InfoWells: The Pause Before the Conclusion

Create a polished 25–35 second vertical video (9:16) for Facebook Reels introducing Signal InfoWells. Audience: people who want to think more clearly before reacting or deciding.

## Creative direction
Deep teal, near-black, warm white, restrained light accents. Calm, premium pacing; clean typography and deliberate motion. Show a fast incoming signal, a pause, six distinct viewpoints, then a clear but open-ended recognition state. Avoid mystical imagery, exaggerated AI claims, fake statistics, clutter, and irrelevant stock footage. Keep text large and readable on a phone; use captions throughout.

## Timeline and on-screen copy
- **0–3s:** “Your first thought isn’t the whole picture.” A signal appears, then motion pauses.
- **3–8s:** “One perspective can miss something important.” One viewpoint opens into several lenses.
- **8–19s:** Reveal one at a time: “Perception.” “Imagination.” “Empathy.” “Introspection.” “Emotion.” “Reason.” Six nodes cross-check; none controls the others.
- **19–26s:** “Know. Believe. Suspect. Don’t know yet.” Four labels appear; uncertainty is not framed as failure.
- **26–33s:** “Pause. Cross-check. Recognize. Then act.” End card: “Signal InfoWells” / “Think beyond the first signal.”

## Voiceover
“Your first thought is a signal, not the whole picture. Signal InfoWells helps you pause, examine a situation through six perspectives, and cross-check what each one reveals. Then be honest about what you know, what you believe, what you suspect, and what you don’t know yet. Think beyond the first signal.”

## Quality gate
Use subtle instrumental music under a clear voiceover. Maintain safe margins, readable captions, and a clean end card. Before export verify: opening is not exaggerated; all six pillars are accurate; Emotion is a movement/signal, not a command; Recognition preserves “Don’t know yet”; no unsupported results are claimed; video works without sound; CTA is readable. Refine any failed criterion before export.
